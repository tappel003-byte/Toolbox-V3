# Toolbox — Governing Principle

> **Measure first. Examine the evidence. Interpret in context. Solve the actual problem.**

Toolbox exists to help the investigator get the answer right, not to sell a predetermined answer. The software collects, preserves, computes, organizes, visualizes, and communicates evidence. Professional interpretation remains with the investigator.

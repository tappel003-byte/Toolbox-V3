What these drawings assume

  • Completed is marked at Check In. The checkmark is what moves a job out of the working drawer and up into Explorer.
  • Filed by the floor survey date — distress survey date if there is no floor survey. No third fallback.
  • Search reads the card catalogue, not the jobs, so it stays instant at five thousand jobs.
  • The map is a view of that catalogue with a date filter on it, not a second store. The coordinates are already being captured.
  • Delete lives here only. A job with work in it can be destroyed from Explorer and nowhere else.
  • The figures in the details pane are computed, not typed — levels, readings, pins, the biggest drop and the span it crosses all come out of the survey when the job is filed.

The one thing these drawings decide that the notes left open: the PDFs and the CSV are real files written when the job is filed, not something rendered when you look at them. That is what makes the ZIP worth anything if Toolbox is gone.
